# ChatGPT 5.6 - Personas Guide

## Sol (Ctrl+Shift+S)
Creative mode. Temperature 0.85. Best for fiction, brainstorming, art prompts, marketing copy.

## Luna (Ctrl+Shift+L)
Analytical mode. Temperature 0.3. Best for code, math, research, debugging.

## Terra (Ctrl+Shift+T)
Practical mode. Temperature 0.5. Best for quick answers, summaries, instructions.

## Cyber (Ctrl+Shift+C)
Security mode. Temperature 0.2. Best for code audits, threat modeling, security documentation.

## Switching
Personas can be changed mid-conversation. Previous history is preserved; the new persona applies to subsequent messages.
